Step 3 — Friend's Solution

A faithful transcription of the handwritten solution, with one small correction noted below.

One correction made

The original first line was "wts: $\int\varphi(L\varphi)\,dz > 0$" (strict). The calculation below establishes $\geq 0$, not strict $> 0$ — the integrand is a nonnegative square, so the integral is $\geq 0$, but proving STRICT $> 0$ for every nonzero $\varphi$ needs an extra contradiction argument (see §6 of the main report). So the goal has been changed to "$\geq 0$" to match what is actually proved.

Setup

Given (Step 2): $h(z) > 0$ on $[-d, d]$.

Want to show:

$$ \int_{-d}^{d}\!\varphi(z)\,(L\varphi)(z)\,dz \;\geq\; 0. $$

Substitute $\varphi = u\cdot h$

Since $h > 0$ on $[-d,d]$, the ratio $\varphi/h$ is well-defined. Define

$$ u(z) \;:=\; \frac{\varphi(z)}{h(z)}, \qquad \varphi(z) \;=\; u(z)\,h(z). $$

Substitute $\varphi = uh$ into the Jacobi operator

$$ L\varphi \;=\; -\varphi'' - q(z)\,\varphi, \qquad q(z) \;=\; \frac{2}{R^2\cosh^2(z/R)}. $$

Compute $\varphi''$ by product rule:

$$ \varphi'' \;=\; (uh)'' \;=\; (u'h + uh')' \;=\; u''h + u'h' + u'h' + uh'' \;=\; u''h + 2u'h' + uh''. $$

Plug into $L\varphi$:

$$ L\varphi \;=\; -\bigl(u''h + 2u'h' + uh''\bigr) - q(z)\,u\,h $$
$$ \phantom{L\varphi} \;=\; -u''h - 2u'h' + u\bigl(-h'' - q(z)\,h\bigr). $$

Since $h$ is a Jacobi field ($Lh = 0$):

$$ Lh \;=\; -h'' - q(z)\,h \;=\; 0. $$

So the bracketed $u\bigl(-h'' - q(z)h\bigr)$ term is just $u\cdot 0 = 0$, leaving:

$$ L\varphi \;=\; -u''h - 2u'h'. $$

Compute $\varphi\,L\varphi$

Multiply $L\varphi$ by $\varphi = uh$:

$$ \varphi\,L\varphi \;=\; (uh)\bigl(-u''h - 2u'h'\bigr) \;=\; -u\,u''\,h^2 \;-\; 2\,u\,u'\,h\,h'. $$

Recognise a total derivative

By the product rule:

$$ \frac{d}{dz}\bigl(u\,u'\,h^2\bigr) \;=\; (uu')'\,h^2 \;+\; u\,u'\cdot(h^2)' \;=\; \bigl((u')^2 + u\,u''\bigr)\,h^2 \;+\; u\,u'\cdot 2hh' $$
$$ \phantom{\frac{d}{dz}\bigl(u\,u'\,h^2\bigr)} \;=\; (u')^2\,h^2 \;+\; u\,u''\,h^2 \;+\; 2\,u\,u'\,h\,h'. $$

The last two terms are exactly the negatives of what appeared in $\varphi L\varphi$. Rearranging:

$$ -u\,u''\,h^2 \;-\; 2\,u\,u'\,h\,h' \;=\; (u')^2\,h^2 \;-\; \frac{d}{dz}\bigl(u\,u'\,h^2\bigr). $$

So:

$$ \varphi\,L\varphi \;=\; (u')^2\,h^2 \;-\; \frac{d}{dz}\bigl(u\,u'\,h^2\bigr). $$

Integrate over $[-d,d]$

$$ \int_{-d}^{d}\!\varphi\,L\varphi\,dz \;=\; \int_{-d}^{d}\! h^2\,(u')^2\,dz \;-\; \bigl[u\,u'\,h^2\bigr]_{-d}^{d}. $$

Boundary term vanishes. Since $\varphi(\pm d) = 0$ (boundary condition from Step 0) and $h(\pm d) > 0$ (Step 2):

$$ u(\pm d) \;=\; \frac{\varphi(\pm d)}{h(\pm d)} \;=\; \frac{0}{h(\pm d)} \;=\; 0. $$

So $u\,u'\,h^2$ at $z = \pm d$ is $0\cdot u'(\pm d)\cdot h(\pm d)^2 = 0$, and the bracket is $0 - 0 = 0$.

Result

Identity
$$ \int_{-d}^{d}\!\varphi\,L\varphi\,dz \;=\; \int_{-d}^{d}\! h^2\,(u')^2\,dz \;=\; \int_{-d}^{d}\! h(z)^2\!\left(\frac{d}{dz}\frac{\varphi(z)}{h(z)}\right)^{\!2}\,dz. $$

Both factors of the integrand are nonnegative:

  • $h(z)^2 \geq 0$ everywhere (always — and $> 0$ here by Step 2).
  • $\bigl(\tfrac{d}{dz}\tfrac{\varphi}{h}\bigr)^2 \geq 0$ (it's a square).

So the integrand is $\geq 0$, hence the integral is $\geq 0$. Therefore:

Stability
$$ A''(0) \;=\; 2\pi R\!\int_{-d}^{d}\!\varphi\,L\varphi\,dz \;\geq\; 0. $$

The outer catenoid is stable (every fixed-boundary perturbation does not decrease area).

To upgrade to strict stability ($A''(0) > 0$ for every $\varphi \not\equiv 0$), see §6 of the main report — short contradiction argument: integral $= 0$ and integrand $\geq 0$ continuous would force $(\varphi/h)' \equiv 0$, then $\varphi = c\,h$, then $\varphi(d) = 0$ forces $c = 0$, contradicting $\varphi \not\equiv 0$.

End of friend's solution
